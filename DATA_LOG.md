## Listed in the challenge resources but not used

| Source | Tier in the plan | Why |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Google Earth Engine | OPTIONAL route | Not used. `earthengine authenticate` had not been run on the build machine, so Landsat was read from Microsoft Planetary Computer instead (the same Landsat Collection 2 Level-2 scenes). Nothing in the app depends on Earth Engine. `python -m pipeline.check_access` reports whether it is ready. |
| ECOSTRESS (land-surface temperature, ET) | SKIP | Skipped. Irregular overpass times and revisit (it rides on the ISS), frequent cloud gaps and heavier swath preprocessing make it a poor fit for a monthly field record; OpenET already supplies field-scale ET from Landsat thermal data. A good add-on for mapping within-field heat hotspots. |
| GRACE / GRACE-FO (water storage) | SKIP | Skipped for field decisions. Its footprint is roughly 300 km, hundreds of times larger than the whole valley, so it cannot say anything about one field or one basin this small. The long-term water trend in the app comes instead from USGS wells inside the valley, which measure the aquifer directly. |
